  2. 联网行为
    • 天气:你搜索城市和查看天气时,应用会向第三方天气/地理编码服务发送所查询的城市名或坐标以获取结果。

  3. 通知:提醒通过系统本地通知发出,不经过任何服务器。
  4. 数据删除:卸载应用并删除其数据目录即可清除本地数据;云端备份由你在自己的服务器上管理。
  5. 儿童:本应用不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的信息。
  6. 变更:政策更新时会在本页面公布。

English

AuraCalendar (时光随记, the “App”) is local-first.

  1. We do not collect personal information. Schedules, to-dos, notes and budget records are stored only in a local database on your device. We operate no servers, create no accounts, and use no analytics or advertising.
  2. Network use
    • Weather: when you search a city or view weather, the App sends the city name or coordinates to third-party weather/geocoding services to fetch results.
    • Cloud sync (optional): only if you configure it, backups and sync data are uploaded to a WebDAV server you specify. With a passphrase set, data is encrypted on your device before upload. The server URL and username are stored locally; the password is stored in the OS credential manager.
  3. Notifications are local system notifications and do not go through any server.
  4. Data deletion: uninstall the App and delete its data folder to remove local data; cloud backups are managed by you on your own server.
  5. Children: the App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
  6. Changes: updates will be posted on this page.
  7. Contact: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com
